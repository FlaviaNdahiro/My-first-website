What makes a form accessible

  • Every control has a <label for="..."> pointing at its id — a for that matches nothing gives the field no name at all.
  • The right type matters: email and tel bring up the correct keyboard on a phone and give you free validation.
  • Radio buttons share one name so the browser treats them as a single choice, and a <fieldset> with a <legend> tells the reader what the choice is for.
  • The first <option> of a required select has an empty value, so “Select a subject category” cannot be submitted as an answer.
  • Focus stays visible — the fields keep a brown outline instead of outline: none, which would leave keyboard users lost.
No JavaScript here. The layout, the two-column fields and the validation messages are all browser behaviour that comes free with correct HTML.
