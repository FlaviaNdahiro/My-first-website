How the restaurant works

Simple instructions for new staff. If you are not sure what to do, come back to this page or ask the manager.

On this page

  1. 1. Taking an order
  2. 2. Booking a table
  3. 3. Taking payment
  4. 4. Handling a complaint
  5. 5. Who does what
  6. 6. Common questions

1. Taking an order

  1. Greet the customer and give them the menu.
  2. Write down every item, including drinks.
  3. Ask about allergies before you send the order.
  4. Repeat the order back to the customer to check it is correct.
  5. Send the ticket to the kitchen.
Tip: see live order tickets on the Kitchen page.

2. Booking a table

Check the Tables page before you seat anyone. A table is either Free, Booked, or Busy.

  • Groups of four or more go to the main hall or family tables.
  • Couples and small groups can sit on the terrace.
  • A booking holds a table for up to two hours at busy times.

3. Taking payment

  1. Print or write the bill once the customer is finished.
  2. Confirm the total with the customer before payment.
  3. Accept cash or mobile money, as agreed by the manager.
  4. Give a receipt and thank the customer.

4. Handling a complaint

Stay calm and listen first. Most complaints are about wait times or a wrong order.
  • Apologise and ask exactly what went wrong.
  • Offer to fix the order or bring it again, free of charge.
  • Call the manager if the customer is still not satisfied.

5. Who does what

Manager
Oversees the restaurant, tables, staff and daily reports.
Chef
Cooks the food and manages the kitchen stations.
Waiter
Takes orders, serves food, and looks after the tables.
Cashier
Prints bills and takes payment from customers.

6. Common questions

Question Answer
A customer wants to change their order Update the ticket and tell the kitchen right away.
All tables are busy Offer to add the customer to the waiting list.
The kitchen runs out of an item Tell the waiter immediately so they can inform customers.
New staff member needs an account Send them to the Staff Registration page.
