Where the food is made

Every order that comes from the dining room is sent to the kitchen. The chefs cook it one ticket at a time, and the waiter takes it to the table as soon as it is ready.

Order tickets

Four tickets on the kitchen board. Ticket 2 is a big party order: it is cooked last and takes the extra space on the board.

Order status

  • Received — the order has reached the kitchen.
  • Cooking — the chef is preparing the food.
  • Ready — waiting for the waiter to pick it up.
  • Served — the food is on the customer's table.
Kitchen rule. Wash your hands before cooking, and always check the allergy notice on the ticket.

Need a table?

Before an order is cooked, a table must be booked. See which tables are free on the Tables page.
