Booking rules

A table is booked by the waiter or by the manager. The customer gets the table for up to two hours at busy times.

Tip: seat groups of four or more in the main hall or at the family tables, and keep the terrace for couples and small groups.

Opening hours

Morning

Tea, coffee, croissants and fresh juice. Mostly small tables and quick service.

Lunch

The busiest time. Salads and pizza are ordered a lot, so book large tables early.

Evening

Relaxed dinners on the terrace and in the main hall, with desserts and hot drinks.
